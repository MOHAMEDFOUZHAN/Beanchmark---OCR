Base Unit Rule in MMS:
All solid materials (Tea, Spices, Fruits, Nuts, Sugar, etc.) are recorded with base unit Kg.
All liquids (Oils, Essences, Flavours) are recorded with base unit Litre.
Because 1 Kg = 1000 Grams and 1 Litre = 1000 ML, quantities are entered using 3 decimal places.

Common Mistake to Avoid:
If you want to transfer 12 grams, do NOT type 12 with unit Kg. Typing 12 will deduct 12 Kilograms (12,000 grams) from stock!
Always type 0.012 for 12 grams.
Benchmark Tea & Chocolate Factory - Module Breakdown:
Here is a guide explaining the purpose of each page in the top navigation bar.

Dashboard

High-level overview showing total inventory count, low stock warnings, expiring materials, and quick daily metrics.

Invoices

Manage raw material purchases and inward bills. Features RapidOCR to automatically scan physical invoice documents and extract line items, prices, and GST.

Transfers / Returns

Issue raw materials to production departments (Tea Factory, Chocolate Factory, Oil Counter, etc.) and record any unused materials returned to inventory.

Materials & Materials Master

The central master catalog of all materials. Search items by code or name, manage unit prices, reorder thresholds, and synchronize units across historical records.

Storage (Batches)

Track inventory by specific Batch / Lot numbers (e.g. BATCH-342), monitor manufacturing & expiry dates, and review inward receipts.

Vendors

Directory of suppliers and raw material vendors, containing contact numbers, GST numbers, addresses, and transaction logs.

Dispatch

Record outward finished products dispatch with automatic FIFO (First-In, First-Out) batch allocation to ensure oldest stock is used first.

Stock Adjustment

Perform manual stock corrections (add or subtract quantity) for physical audit discrepancies with mandatory reason logging.

Shelf Mapping

Map material categories to specific physical warehouse shelves, racks, and bin locations for fast warehouse retrieval.

MUR & Reports

Access deep analytics: Material Utilization Reports (MUR), Departmental Usage Breakdown, Daily Inventory Ledgers, Expiring Items, and Reorder alerts.
