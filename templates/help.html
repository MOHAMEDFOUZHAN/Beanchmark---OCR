1. How to Enter Grams & Millilitres (ML)

Base Unit Concept: In MMS, all weights are tracked in Kg (1 Kg = 1000 g) and liquids in Litre (1 L = 1000 ml). Enter smaller quantities as decimals with 3 places.

2. System Pages & Features Guide

Dashboard

Real-time KPI metrics: active lots, inventory value, low-stock warnings, and expiring material alerts.

Invoices

Purchase inward registration. Features RapidOCR scanning to automatically digitize physical supplier bills.

Transfers / Returns

Issue raw materials to production departments (Tea Factory, Chocolate Factory, etc.) and record returned stock.

Materials & Materials Master

Master catalog of all raw materials, pricing, reorder thresholds, and historical unit synchronization.

Storage (Batches)

Lot-level tracking with manufacturing and expiry dates, inward history, and real-time available stock.

Vendors

Supplier master directory containing contact information, GST numbers, and purchase transaction histories.

Dispatch

Record finished goods dispatches with automated FIFO batch deduction.

Stock Adjustment

Manual inventory corrections with mandatory audit logging and reason records.

Shelf Mapping

Map material categories to specific physical warehouse shelves, racks, and bins.

MUR & Reports

Material Utilization Reports, Departmental Transfers, Daily Ledgers, and Expiring Batches warnings.
